Process

How does the collaboration work?

1) Request, initial assessment and quote

You describe the problem or goal. I check whether I can help and what information I need. Before any paid analysis or implementation begins, I confirm the proposed scope and cost.

2) Advice or hands-on work

If the issue can be resolved without access to your system, I analyse the materials you provide and send you written recommendations to implement yourself. If direct work in Home Assistant is required, we agree on a secure access method, usually a separate service account and, if necessary and with your consent, a remote-support tool. For a new installation, we first start the system and you create your own administrator account. I only make the agreed changes, we test the result, and you remove the service access when the work is complete.
