About Me

Process

How does the collaboration work?

1) Request, diagnosis, and quote

You describe the problem or goal. I check whether I can help, then present the proposed scope and cost of the work.

2) Implementation, testing, and completion

If Home Assistant is already running, you create a separate service account for the duration of the work. For a new installation, we first start the system and you create your own administrator account, followed by a separate service account for further configuration. If needed, and with your consent, I install a supporting tool. I only make the agreed changes, we test the results together, and you delete the service account when the work is complete.
