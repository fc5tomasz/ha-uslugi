Services for businesses

Home Assistant can make it easier to manage devices, rooms and energy use across your business.

I configure Home Assistant remotely, integrate compatible devices, and set up automations and practical control dashboards. I tailor the work to your business needs and what your existing equipment can support.

Practical applications

Every property has different needs. Here are a few ways Home Assistant can be used in practice.

Hotels and guesthouses

Monitor room temperatures, set heating schedules, check whether windows are open and keep track of energy use, depending on what the property's equipment supports.

Holiday rentals and short-stay apartments

Get rooms to a comfortable temperature before guests arrive, reduce heating in empty spaces and check devices remotely. Schedules can use booking information if the booking system makes it available.

Offices

Control lighting, blinds and compatible windows. Manage workday scenes, schedules and room temperatures from one place.

Shops, salons and other business premises

Set up opening and closing scenes, lighting and heating schedules, and a clear overview of selected devices.

Energy monitoring

View current and historical energy use from compatible meters, compare usage during business hours and receive alerts about unusual consumption.

Managing multiple properties

See selected information from different locations in one place, including temperatures, device status and energy use. The setup depends on the available infrastructure.
