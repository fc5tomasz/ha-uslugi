HA Expert Privacy Policy

2. Purposes of processing and legal bases

HA Expert processes data for the following purposes:

  • handling enquiries submitted through the contact form or by email and taking steps at the user's request before entering into a contract or in connection with performing a contract, under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR;
  • handling other correspondence, ensuring website security and preventing abuse, where necessary for the legitimate interests of HA Expert, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR;
  • measuring traffic and advertising effectiveness using Google Analytics and Google Ads, only after the relevant consent has been given, under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR;
  • operating HA Expert's own analytics described in section 4, only after consent for the “analytics” purpose has been given. To the extent that the information processed constitutes personal data, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR;
  • complying with legal obligations, in particular accounting and tax obligations connected with a contract, under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

The website and the contact form also work without consent to analytics or marketing purposes. Refusing consent does not restrict access to the offer or the ability to send an enquiry.

3. Data that may be processed

Depending on how the website is used, the following data may be processed:

  • data voluntarily provided through the contact form or by email, in particular contact details and the content of an enquiry;
  • technical data necessary to deliver and secure the website, which may be processed by infrastructure providers, such as an IP address, HTTP request information and basic connection data;
  • data related to Google Analytics and Google Ads, only in accordance with the user's consent choices;
  • HA Expert's own analytics data described in section 4.

Important distinction: infrastructure providers may technically process an IP address in order to deliver and secure the website, while HA Expert's own analytics database does not store the raw IP address.

4. HA Expert's own analytics

HA Expert's own analytics is used to produce anonymous statistics about how the website is used. HA Expert does not use it to determine who a visitor is or to recognise the same person across separate visits.

After consent to the “analytics” purpose has been given, the system may record:

  • a random session identifier stored only in sessionStorage for the current browser tab;
  • the event time;
  • the website language;
  • a sanitised URL path without query parameters or URL fragments;
  • the traffic source, for example a search engine, an advertisement or a direct visit, and only the presence of selected campaign parameters without storing their values;
  • the country supplied by the infrastructure layer on the server side, without storing the raw IP address in HA Expert analytics;
  • active time on the page;
  • scroll depth;
  • viewed sections;
  • named actions on the page, for example clicking the contact form.

HA Expert's own analytics does not store:

  • the visitor's first or last name;
  • an email address;
  • the content or data entered into the contact form;
  • the raw IP address;
  • the full User-Agent string;
  • a persistent identifier that would allow the same person to be recognised across separate visits;
  • mouse movements;
  • session recordings.

The random session identifier is used only to connect events within one session of the current browser tab and is not used to identify a person.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

HA Expert uses a consent mechanism that allows users to decide separately about the “analytics” and “marketing” purposes.

  • ha_consent_v2 is stored in the browser's localStorage and is used only to remember the user's consent choices. It remains stored until the choice is changed, the website data is cleared in the browser, or the consent mechanism is reset by the website;
  • the session identifier used by HA Expert's own analytics is stored in sessionStorage and is no longer available after that browser tab's session ends;
  • Google Analytics and Google Ads may use cookies and similar technologies in accordance with the consent given and Google Consent Mode settings;
  • technologies strictly necessary for the operation or security of the website, or for remembering privacy settings, may operate without analytics or marketing consent.

Current information about the technologies used by Google and their retention periods is published in Google's official documentation at policies.google.com/technologies/cookies.

6. Recipients and service providers

To the extent necessary for the website to operate, data may be processed by:

  • Google Analytics and Google Ads (Google Ireland Limited), for traffic and advertising effectiveness measurement in accordance with the consent given. How Google processes data is described in the Google Privacy Policy;
  • Cloudflare, as the provider of infrastructure, CDN, website security and the services used by the contact form backend. The processing terms are set out in the Cloudflare Data Processing Addendum;
  • Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.), as the provider of email delivery related to the contact form. The processing terms are set out in the Resend Data Processing Addendum;
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc.), for hosting the static part of the website on GitHub Pages. How GitHub processes data is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement;
  • technical infrastructure providers necessary for the proper and secure operation of the services, only to the extent required to provide those services.

Results from HA Expert's own analytics are used by HA Expert and are not shared with Google Ads or other parties for advertising profiling.

7. Google Consent Mode

Before consent is given, the relevant Google consent signals (ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization, analytics_storage) are set to “denied”. After the user makes a choice, the settings are updated according to the consent given.

Depending on how Google Consent Mode operates, Google may receive limited technical or measurement signals without the relevant cookies when consent has not been given. Google determines how these signals operate.

HA Expert's own analytics remains completely disabled if the user does not consent to the “analytics” purpose.

8. Retention periods

  • HA Expert's own analytics data is stored for a maximum of 180 days from the event and is then automatically deleted;
  • the decision stored in ha_consent_v2 remains in the browser until it is changed, website data is cleared, or the consent mechanism is reset;
  • correspondence and contact form data is retained for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry, perform the cooperation and protect against potential legal claims;
  • data that must be retained under accounting, tax or other applicable laws is retained for the period required by law;
  • data processed by external providers may be subject to their documented retention periods depending on the type of service and applicable agreements.

HA Expert does not retain data longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected unless further retention is required by law.

9. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some providers used by HA Expert may process data outside the European Economic Area, particularly in the United States.

Google uses legal mechanisms for international data transfers, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where required. Details are described in Google's documentation on data transfer frameworks.

Cloudflare uses the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and appropriate Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers requiring an additional legal safeguard, as set out in Cloudflare's GDPR information.

Resend primarily processes customer data in the United States. Transfers from the European Economic Area are covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated into the Resend Data Processing Addendum and by Resend's participation in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as set out in the Resend Data Processing Addendum.

GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries. For transfers from the European Economic Area it relies, among other mechanisms, on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses, as set out in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. User rights

Where provided for by the GDPR, the user has the right to:

  • receive information about the processing of their data;
  • access their data;
  • rectify their data;
  • erase their data;
  • restrict processing;
  • data portability;
  • object to processing where the legal basis is a legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

Consent for the “analytics” and “marketing” purposes can be changed or withdrawn at any time using the privacy settings available on the website. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it and does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

The user has the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, in particular Datatilsynet in Denmark or the data protection authority competent for the user's habitual residence.

HA Expert's own analytics does not contain a persistent identifier or data that enables HA Expert to recognise a particular person across separate visits. For this reason, it may be technically impossible to determine which analytics events relate to a particular person based solely on that person's identity. HA Expert will not collect additional data solely to identify a person unless required by applicable law.

HA Expert does not use website data to make decisions about users based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect them.
