Home Assistant

What is Home Assistant?

Author: Tomasz Furdal · HA Expert

Home Assistant is a free, open-source home automation platform that connects devices from different manufacturers in one locally managed system. Instead of using many separate apps, you can control them from one place and create automations tailored to everyday needs. A major advantage of Home Assistant is local control. This allows many devices to work faster, more reliably, and independently of external servers or the internet. If you also use cloud-based devices, Home Assistant can integrate them with the rest of your home under one convenient interface.

FEATURES

What can this system do?

LIGHTING 💡

Lighting, blinds and daily routines

Lights can respond to motion, brightness, time of day and whether anyone is home. Blinds can close during strong sunlight, while one routine can turn off the lights, close the blinds and activate night mode.

SECURITY 🛡️

Security and monitoring

Home Assistant can connect a compatible alarm, cameras, motion sensors, door and window sensors, and locks. When the last person leaves, the system can arm the alarm, turn off selected devices and send a notification about a detected event. The available functions depend on the devices and integrations.

COMFORT 🌡️

Heating, cooling and ventilation

Temperature can be adjusted according to the time of day, occupancy, open windows and weather conditions. Home Assistant can work with thermostats, underfloor heating, heat pumps, air conditioning and ventilation when the devices provide a suitable integration.

ENERGY ⚡

Energy and utility consumption

The system can display grid consumption, solar production, battery operation and the energy use of selected devices. Available measurements can be used to run appliances during high solar production or at times when electricity prices are lower.

WATER 💧

Water monitoring and leak protection

The system can monitor leak sensors, water flow and the state of a compatible valve. When a leak is detected, it can send a notification and, with a suitable installation, also shut off the water supply. The available functions depend on the devices and integrations in use.

OUTDOORS 🌿

Garden and outdoor areas

Home Assistant can control lighting around the garden, driveway and terrace and work with irrigation, pumps and moisture sensors. An automation can pause watering when it rains or send a notification about unusual water consumption.

VOICE AND AI 🎙️

Voice control and artificial intelligence

Home Assistant can handle voice commands for lights, blinds, temperature, media and prepared routines. Some functions can operate locally, while others use cloud services. AI integration is optional and depends on the configuration and available hardware.

DASHBOARDS 📱

Custom control dashboards

Different views can be prepared for phones, tablets and computers. A wall-mounted dashboard can show the status of the home, a phone can provide quick access to controls, and a simplified view can be created for children or guests.

MEDIA 🎬

Media and prepared routines

The system can connect a television, receiver, speakers and media players within prepared routines. Activating movie mode can dim the lights, close the blinds and adjust selected devices. The available actions depend on the integrations provided by each device.

ADVANTAGE

Why Home Assistant instead of standard hubs?

Logic 🧩

Advanced operating logic

Manufacturer apps usually only allow simple rules. Home Assistant gives you much more freedom: you can combine multiple conditions, dependencies, and exceptions in a single automation, exactly how your home works.

Independence 🔒

Privacy and independence

Many standard systems rely on the manufacturer's cloud. If the service goes down or the manufacturer drops support, some features might simply disappear. Home Assistant allows you to maintain more control over the system and become independent of such limitations.

Open Source 🌍

Openness

Home Assistant is developed by a huge community that constantly creates new integrations and extensions. As a result, the system quickly adapts to new devices, standards, and control methods.

HARDWARE

What can it run on?

Home Assistant does not require an expensive server. Energy-efficient thin clients, mini PCs, some NAS servers, and even older laptops can work well.

A popular device for running Home Assistant is the Raspberry Pi. If you want to begin yourself, follow the complete guide for Raspberry Pi 4 and 5.

Where to download and how to get started?
